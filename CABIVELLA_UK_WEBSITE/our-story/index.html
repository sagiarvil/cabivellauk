WHERE THE IDEA BEGINS

Considered furniture.
For lived-in rooms.

Cabivella is a name for a simple idea: well-designed cabinetry should make daily life easier without losing a sense of character. The design direction draws on furniture-making traditions in Turkey and the measured, enduring appeal of British interiors.

We are developing a collection-led approach to kitchens, bathrooms, pantries, coffee corners and bespoke storage. Each is a different kind of space, but the questions are familiar: What needs to be stored? How should it be reached? Which details will matter after years of everyday use?

This website presents the intended collection structure and design approach. Details of the legal trading entity, product availability, manufacturing arrangements and UK service coverage must be verified before commercial launch.

ISTANBUL TO THE UK

Respect for making.
Space for living.

Our visual story draws on Istanbul as a source of architectural character and on the practical expectations of UK homes. Rather than claiming an invented heritage, we believe the work should eventually speak through real materials, accurate specifications and documented projects.
