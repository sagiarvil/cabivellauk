CABIVELLA — UK MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE V6

Start here: open index.html, or publish this entire folder on a static host.
The actual homepage in this release has the dark green utility bar, Cabivella crest + wordmark, photograph-led "More room for living" hero, and six linked collection tiles matching the approved design reference.

Includes 28 content pages: 6 main collections, 14 product-design details, and supporting information/enquiry pages.
All visible website UI is written in British English. No prices and no invented contact details.

NOTE: Photography is visual design/reference material; it must not be represented as completed customer projects. The approved homepage mock-up was used to crop design visualisations for the homepage.
The enquiry form remains preview-only until the actual company's contact details, UK privacy/legal requirements and form delivery are configured. Avoid advertising a UK showroom/phone number until real.
Product pages are design directions until final product SKUs/specifications and licensed photographs are supplied.

For local testing: python3 -m http.server 8000 (from this folder) -> http://localhost:8000/
For Netlify: deploy contents of this folder as the site root.
