COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you begin

What is a Shaker kitchen? +

Shaker cabinetry is recognised for framed door fronts with a recessed centre panel. Finishes and hardware can create a traditional or contemporary look.

Do you supply kitchen appliances? +

Appliance selection and installation depend on the agreed project scope; please specify your requirements when you enquire.
