Questions about project supply

Can I buy cabinetry from Turkey for a European project?

Cabivella accepts project enquiries involving made-to-measure cabinetry manufactured in Turkey. Destination, delivery scope and commercial terms are confirmed individually during quotation.

Do you support trade quantities?

Trade buyers can submit volume requirements. Quantity-based pricing, production scheduling and documentation are assessed for each project, not assumed.

Do you provide prices on the website?

Cabivella cabinetry is quoted to the agreed specification. Send measurements or drawings to request itemised pricing.

Do you install cabinetry in my country?

Installation arrangements are project- and destination-dependent. Do not assume installation is included until your written quotation confirms it.
