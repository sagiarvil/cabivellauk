PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION & DUTY OF CARE

The evidence
behind the material.

Construction products cannot be treated as interchangeable commodities. Applicable UK and EU requirements vary by product, intended use and destination. Our enquiry process identifies what must be checked; it does not imply that every listed product is certified or suitable for every project.

Requests involving structural, fire, electrical, potable-water or other safety-critical applications are subject to heightened product-specific verification. Independent engineering and compliance review may be required before purchase.

UK construction products guidance ↗EU CPR overview ↗
01
Product identification

Manufacturer, model, dimensions, composition, intended use, technical datasheet and traceability.

02
Applicable performance evidence

Declaration of performance/conformity, relevant test evidence and marking only where the law and product scope require them.

03
Samples and finish approval

Colour, surface treatment, tolerances, batch matching and approval process agreed as required.

04
Packing and transport

Crate or pallet configuration, handling constraints, protection, insurance and inspection responsibilities.

05
Commercial clarity

Incoterms® if agreed, importer of record, customs documentation, duties, VAT and delivery boundary confirmed in the quote.
