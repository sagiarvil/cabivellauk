International delivery starts with a precise specification.

Cabivella welcomes made-to-measure cabinetry enquiries from the United Kingdom and European countries. Final destinations, freight arrangements and responsibilities are agreed case by case. This page is a guide to what buyers should clarify before placing an order.

What buyers should confirm

01. Product scope

Final drawings, measurements, materials, quantities and agreed specifications.

02. Commercial terms

Quotation currency, payment milestones, Incoterms (if agreed), taxes and importer responsibilities.

03. Delivery & aftercare

Destination, packaging, carrier, delivery boundary, inspection and installation scope.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cabivella export cabinetry from Turkey?

Cabivella is positioned as a Turkey-based cabinetry exporter serving enquiries from the UK and Europe. Delivery feasibility, responsibilities and documents are established with each quotation.

Are import duties and VAT included?

No blanket duty or tax inclusion is stated. The written quote must identify the importer, destination, applicable tax treatment and delivery terms.

Who arranges installation?

The site currently describes UK kerbside delivery and installation by a chosen contractor. Any additional service must be explicitly included in the agreed project scope.

How can buyers request transport pricing?

Send the product dimensions, approximate quantities and full destination country or postcode through Cabivella’s enquiry channel to receive a project-specific response.

Share your destination and drawings.

We assess each enquiry on its actual requirements, rather than presenting unverified blanket shipping promises.
