## Verified Residential Joinery & Commercial MEP Standards
- BS 6222-2: British Standard specification for structural performance, shelf load bearing, and hinge cycle durability for domestic fitted cabinetry, bespoke cupboards, and fitted wooden storage.
- EN 13986 & FSC / PEFC Timber: Wood-based structural cores and sustainably harvested European oak, ash, and walnut veneers with Class E1 formaldehyde emissions under 0.1 ppm.
- Blum Certified Motion Hardware: Integrated Blumotion hinges and Legrabox runners tested to 200,000 opening and closing cycles (50 kg dynamic drawer load).
- BS EN 61537 & BS 7671: Mechanical load and earth-continuity compliance for perforated steel cable containment, heavy-duty cable ladders, wire mesh baskets, and IP4X trunking supplied to London and UK M&E sites.
- EN 1634-1 & BS 476 Part 22: Certified E60, EI60, and EI120 fire resistance for value engineered pre-hung steel service riser doors and technical plant room access sets.
- BS EN 13030 & Qualicoat Class 2 Seaside: Airflow, rain-defence, and marine-grade powder-coating standards for extruded EN AW-6063 T6 architectural aluminium louvre systems.
