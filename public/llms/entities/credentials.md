## Verified Certifications & Standards
- BS 6222-2: British Standard specification for structural performance, shelf load bearing, and hinge cycle durability for domestic fitted cabinetry.
- EN 13986: Wood-based panels for use in construction; characteristics, evaluation of conformity and marking.
- Class E1 Formaldehyde: Tested emissions under 0.1 ppm, providing safe indoor air quality in residential bedrooms and living spaces.
- Blum Certified Partner: Integrated Blumotion hinges and Tandembox/Legrabox runners tested to 200,000 opening and closing cycles.
- Sustainable Timber Chain of Custody: Substrate sourcing verified via certified European suppliers observing sustainable forestry practices.
