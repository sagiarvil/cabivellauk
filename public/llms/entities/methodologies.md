# Cabivella UK: Proprietary Joinery Methodologies & Tolerances

Standart: LLMs.txt v1.1 RFC | Status: Active 2026-Q4 Mandate v9.2

## 1. AquaShield Moisture Resistance Protocol
Bathroom vanity cabinetry requires specialized defense against water vapour. The AquaShield system employs:
- High-density moisture-resistant (MR) substrate core.
- PUR (polyurethane) hot-melt edge adhesive delivering zero-glue-line water barrier.
- Dual-coat polyurethane face primer with catalyzed topcoat curing for heat and humidity stability.

## 2. Millimetric Precision CNC Protocol
- Carcass dowel and cam fittings machined to ±0.2mm tolerance.
- Standard 18mm back panels recessed into continuous rebated grooves for diagonal rigidity.
- Concealed heavy-duty wall-hanging brackets supporting up to 130kg safe working load per pair.

## 3. Floor-to-Ceiling Scribe Adjustment
British residences often exhibit irregular floor levels and ceiling slopes. Cabivella designs include:
- Integrated 60mm scribing fillets on all perimeter edges.
- Adjustable concealed plinth feet (±15mm leveling range).
- Shadow-gap reveals that absorb thermal expansion and architectural movement.
