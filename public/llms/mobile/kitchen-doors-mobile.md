## Quick Answer (Hero Engine)
Cabivella custom replacement doors allow homeowners to refresh existing kitchen carcasses with premium architectural facades. Each panel is precision-cut to custom dimensions and pre-drilled to match specified 35mm cup hinge positions. Finishes include moisture-resistant primed MDF for on-site finishing, durable polyurethane lacquer, and natural oak or walnut veneers.

## Mobile Decision Support
- Q: Can replacement doors fit IKEA Metod or Faktum units?
- A: Yes, hinge hole positions and drill centres are machined to exact IKEA dimensions or custom bespoke carcass measurements.

- Q: Are doors supplied painted or primed?
- A: Both options are available: fully finished in factory polyurethane lacquer or supplied in smooth 2-coat primer ready for on-site hand-painting.
