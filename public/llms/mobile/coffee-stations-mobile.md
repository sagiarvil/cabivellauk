## Quick Answer (Hero Engine)
Cabivella integrated coffee stations enclose professional espresso machines behind effortless bi-folding pocket facades. Each station features heat-resistant composite stone backsplashes, passive rear convection channels to prevent moisture entrapment, integrated electrical conduit feeds, and dedicated storage for glassware and artisanal coffee bean canisters.

## Mobile Decision Support
- Q: Can a plumbed espresso machine be installed inside?
- A: Yes, the rear service cavity accommodates dedicated flexible water supply lines, drainage tubing and power sockets.

- Q: How does Cabivella prevent steam buildup inside coffee stations?
- A: Rear and upper ventilation conduits ensure passive convection airflow, preventing steam condensation on interior joinery.
