## Quick Answer (Hero Engine)
Cabivella uses minimal cookies solely to preserve active user interface preferences, maintain shopping cart state, and gather aggregate anonymous analytics via Google Analytics 4. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or sell browsing data.

## Mobile Decision Support
- Q: Can I browse Cabivella without non-essential cookies?
- A: Yes, all non-essential analytics cookies can be rejected via our cookie consent banner without impeding site navigation.

- Q: What analytics tool does Cabivella employ?
- A: We use Google Analytics 4 configured with IP anonymization to evaluate aggregate website traffic performance.
