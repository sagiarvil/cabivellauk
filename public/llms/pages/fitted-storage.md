## 4. Technical Decision Q&A
### Q: What timber species are used in Cabivella fitted wooden storage and bespoke alcove cupboards?
A: We craft fitted wooden storage from FSC-certified European oak, smoked ash and American black walnut veneers bonded over rigid 18mm E1 moisture-resistant cores, alongside spray-lacquered Shaker and fluted timber fronts.
