# Cabivella — Plain British English examples for editorial approval

These are examples of **wording style only**. Do not publish without confirming the feature and fulfilment terms. No invented production claims, factory dates, case studies, prices or customer reviews.

## Page title / CTA
Before: `Enquire a Project`
After: `Enquire About a Project`

Before: `Request a Tailored Quote for the Mayfair 154cm Artisan Beverage Credenza`
After: `Enquire About This Coffee Station Cabinet`

## Coffee Station
**Heading:** Coffee station cabinets
**Subheading:** A place for your coffee machine, mugs and everyday essentials.
**Body:** Explore cabinet layouts with shelves, drawers and room for your chosen appliances. Tell us the available space and how you use it, and we can discuss a suitable configuration. Door mechanisms, finishes, services and installation requirements are confirmed before a quotation is issued.
**Button:** Enquire About This Design
**Avoid:** stating a pocket-door mechanism, quartz top, wiring or built-in plumbing for a model unless its specific design sheet confirms those options.

## Shaker Kitchens
**Heading:** Shaker kitchens
**Body:** Shaker doors suit period houses, modern extensions and everyday family kitchens. Explore framed and painted door styles, practical cupboard layouts and different storage arrangements. Contact us with your room measurements to discuss a design that can be supplied to your specification.
**Button:** Discuss Your Kitchen
**Avoid:** claiming FSC wood, solid ash or any branded hinge in every model without material/parts evidence.

## Wall-Hung Bathroom Cabinetry
**Heading:** Wall-hung vanity units
**Body:** Wall-hung bathroom furniture helps keep the floor clear and makes smaller rooms easier to clean. Select a preferred width and finish, then share your wall construction, basin requirements and plumbing position. Suitability and fixing details must be checked for the proposed installation.
**Button:** Enquire About a Vanity Unit
**Avoid:** absolute claims that every model is waterproof or fits any wall, or that mounting is certified without test data.

## Our Story
**Heading:** Cabinetry for homes across the UK
**Paragraph:** Cabivella presents a range of kitchen, bathroom and storage cabinetry for homeowners, designers and trade customers. We discuss individual room requirements and confirm the proposed materials, manufacture, delivery arrangements and fitting responsibilities before an order is agreed.
**Disclosure gate:** Add the actual contracting legal entity and the true manufacturing location only after the commercial owner verifies them. If made in Turkey and sold to UK customers, say that plainly. Do not use an imaginary London studio or royal endorsement.

## Real British product vocabulary
- `made-to-measure kitchen doors`, not `architecturally immortal kitchen front systems`
- `pantry cupboards` / `larder cupboards`, not every time `culinary sanctuaries`
- `coffee station cabinet` / `breakfast cupboard`, not repeated `artisan beverage credenza`
- `utility room cupboards` and `fitted wardrobes`, not vague `architectural storage masterpieces`
- `painted`, `veneered`, `laminate`, `oak`, `MR-MDF` only where documented
- `matt`, `colour`, `customised`, `galvanised`, `levelling`, `organised` in ordinary UK copy

## Acceptance: UK editors
Every page must answer, in natural concise English: What is the item? Which room? What size options are confirmed? What is included / excluded? How does the customer enquire? What is known and unknown about delivery, fitting and warranty?
